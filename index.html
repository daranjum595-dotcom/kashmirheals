Who we are

Born from resilience.
Built on empathy.

Kashmir Heals began with a simple belief: that healing is not a private struggle. It is a shared journey. Inspired by the extraordinary resilience of the Kashmiri people — who have held beauty and pain in the same breath for generations — we created a space where conversations about mental health, identity, and belonging can happen freely.

We are not a clinic or a helpline. We are a community — of listeners, creators, survivors, and supporters. We believe that art, honest dialogue, and human warmth are among the most powerful medicines there are.

Whether you come carrying something heavy, or simply want to be present for others — you are welcome here.

What we do

Three pillars. One intention.

Conversations

We hold regular facilitated circles where people can speak openly about what they carry — grief, anxiety, identity, loss, hope. No judgment. No agenda. Just listening.

Art & Expression

Sometimes words aren't enough. Through writing, music, visual art, and storytelling, we offer pathways to process and express what lives deep inside us.

Community

Healing doesn't happen in isolation. We build lasting bonds — between strangers who become companions, between those who have healed and those still on their way.
